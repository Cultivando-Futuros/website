For families

A free, paid science program for your teen

We are not accepting applications yet. Check back soon: we will post how to apply when the program opens.

1. What is it?

Cultivando Futuros is a free program, about 15 months long, for high school students in the Salinas and Pajaro Valleys. Your teen will work on a small team with college students from UC Santa Cruz and UC Berkeley and with scientists. Together, they will study a health or environment issue in your community.

2. How much does it cost?

Nothing. It is free. Each student also gets a $2,000 stipend, paid through Clínica de Salud del Valle de Salinas. Check with CSVS whether the payment needs a Social Security number or ITIN for tax forms. If it does, say so here.

3. How much time does it take?

  • Team meetings every two weeks, after school.
  • Guest speaker talks every two weeks in the spring.
  • Research activities, mostly in the summer.
  • A final presentation at the end of the program.

The program does not provide transportation. Your teen will need a way to get to activities in Salinas or nearby.

4. Who will your teen work with?

Scientists, health workers, and community leaders from our partners: UC Berkeley, UC Santa Cruz, CSU Monterey Bay, Clínica de Salud del Valle de Salinas, and the Center for Community Advocacy. College students will serve as mentors. We will add information about safety training and background checks for adults once confirmed.

5. See your teen's work

You and your family will be invited to bilingual events where students present their research.

6. What do we need from you?

  • Sign your teen's application.
  • If your teen is chosen, sign a permission form so they can take part.

7. Your privacy

  • Our application never asks about immigration status or Social Security numbers.
  • Questions about your family's work or education are optional.
  • Only the program's Steering Committee sees the applications.

8. The research study is your choice

Your teen applies only for the program. As part of joining, your teen will be invited to also take part in a research study about the program. Your teen can be in the program without joining the study, with the same activities and the same stipend. Taking part is up to you and your teen. Saying no will not affect whether your teen is chosen, or the stipend. Learn more about the study →

9. Questions? Call us.

Our Field Coordinator speaks English and Spanish and can answer your questions. When applications open, the Field Coordinator can also help you fill out the application.
