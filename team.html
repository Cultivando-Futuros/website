4. Our partners

  • UC Berkeley School of Public Health leads the program and the CHAMACOS study.
  • UC Santa Cruz Global and Community Health program: college student mentors.
  • CSU Monterey Bay: faculty mentor, and connections with local school districts for high school outreach.
  • Clínica de Salud del Valle de Salinas: a community health center serving farmworker families. High school stipends are paid through the clinic.
  • Center for Community Advocacy: a community organization rooted in farmworker families. Its role in youth outreach is being updated.
