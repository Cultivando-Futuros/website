For high school students

Is this program for you?

We are not accepting applications yet. Check back soon: we will post how to apply when the program opens.

1. You can apply if:

  • You are in 10th or 11th grade.
  • You go to high school in the Salinas or Pajaro Valley.
  • You are curious about science, health, or the environment.
  • You can come to team meetings and guest speaker talks every two weeks, and do research in the summer.
  • You speak and understand both English and Spanish.
  • Your parent or guardian says it's okay.

You do not need good grades or test scores. You do not need any research experience, either.

2. What you get

  • A $2,000 stipend. We pay you so you can take part instead of working.
  • Real research experience you can talk about in college and job applications.
  • Mentors from UC Santa Cruz and UC Berkeley who want to help you succeed.
  • A chance to be heard. You will share your findings with local leaders.
  • New friends who care about the same things you do.

3. What we ask of you

  • Come to team meetings every two weeks, after school.
  • Come to guest speaker talks every two weeks in the spring.
  • Do research activities, mostly in the summer.
  • Present your project to the community at the end of the program.
  • Stay with the program until the end, about 15 months.

Getting there: We do not provide rides. You will need your own way to get to activities.

4. How applying will work

  1. Fill out a short application. It asks about you, why you want to join, and which team you like best. There are no right or wrong answers. You can fill it out online or on paper.
  2. Ask a parent or guardian to sign it.
  3. Turn it in by the deadline.

We are not accepting applications yet. Check back soon. We will post how to apply here when the program opens.

5. What happens next

  • After the deadline, the program's Steering Committee reads every application.
  • Our Field Coordinator calls your family to tell you our decision.
  • If you are chosen, you are invited to a welcome event.

6. Questions students ask

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free. And you get paid.

Do I have to be great at science?

No. You just need to be curious.

I work after school. Can I still apply when applications open?

Yes. Tell us on your application. It will not hurt your chances. The stipend is there so you can take part.

Can I pick my team?

You rank the teams from first to last. We will try to give you one of your top choices.

Will you ask about immigration status?

No. The application never asks about it.

Is there a separate application for the study?

No. There is only one application, for the program.

Do I have to join the research study?

No. You apply only for the program. After you join, you will be invited to also take part in the study. It is your choice. You can be in the program without the study, with the same activities and the same stipend.

What if many students apply?

If we have more applicants than spots, older students get the first spots. Younger students go on a waitlist.
