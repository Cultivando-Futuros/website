For UC Berkeley graduate students

Advise youth research teams as a research consultant

Bring your research training to high school students and college mentors studying health and the environment in the Salinas and Pajaro Valleys.

We are not accepting applications yet. We expect applications to open in February 2027. Notify me when the application opens →

1. The role at a glance

  • $500 stipend, paid through UC Berkeley
  • 4 research consultant positions in each group, 1 on each research team
  • The full program, about 15 months
  • Mostly remote
  • Whether this can count toward degree requirements or independent study credit is being confirmed.

2. What you'll do

  • Advise one of four teams: epidemiology, environmental sciences, medical science, or science education.
  • Hold regular office hours for your team's undergraduate mentors and high school students. Remote is fine.
  • Join team meetings every two weeks, as needed.
  • Visits to the Salinas and Pajaro Valleys and guest speaker talks are optional.
  • Gain experience in community-engaged research and mentoring.
  • Epidemiology
  • Environmental sciences
  • Medical science
  • Science education

3. Who can apply

  • Graduate students in the UC Berkeley School of Public Health Environmental Health Sciences and Epidemiology; eligible programs to be confirmed.
  • You speak and understand both English and Spanish.
  • You can commit to the full program, about 15 months.
  • You will not graduate before the program ends.
  • Students from farming or Latine communities are especially encouraged to apply.

4. Built on CHAMACOS

The program builds on more than 25 years of partnership between UC Berkeley and Salinas Valley families through the CHAMACOS study, which has produced more than 200 scientific papers.

5. How applying will work

  1. Fill out a short online application with a few short-answer questions.
  2. Attach your CV.
  3. The Steering Committee reads every application and may invite you to a short interview.

6. The research study

You apply only for the research consultant role. As part of joining, you will be invited to also take part in a research study about the program. It is optional: you can be a research consultant without joining the study. Saying no will not affect whether you are chosen, your stipend, grades, or academic standing. Learn more about the study →
