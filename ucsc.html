For UCSC undergraduate students

Mentor high school students in community health research

Guide a small team of high school students from the Salinas and Pajaro Valleys as they plan and carry out a community research project.

We are not accepting applications yet. Check back soon: we will post how to apply when the program opens.

1. The role at a glance

  • $1,750 stipend, paid through UC Santa Cruz
  • 8 mentor positions in each group, 2 on each research team
  • The full program, about 15 months, including summer
  • Whether this can count toward the GCH internship requirement is being confirmed.

2. What you'll do

  • Serve as a near-peer mentor on one of four teams: epidemiology, environmental sciences, medical science, or science education.
  • Work side by side with 3 or 4 high school students, a UC Berkeley graduate research consultant, and faculty and community mentors.
  • Come to team meetings every two weeks, and to guest speaker talks every two weeks in the spring.
  • Build your own research, leadership, and mentoring skills. We call this "mentoring the mentor."

3. Who can apply

  • Declared Global and Community Health majors (BS or BA), ideally in your third year.
  • You speak and understand both English and Spanish.
  • You can commit to the full program, about 15 months, including summer.
  • You will not graduate before the program ends.
  • Students from farming or Latine communities are especially encouraged to apply.

4. Getting there

Travel: Some team meetings are in person in the Salinas and Pajaro Valleys. You will travel there about once a month, and more often in summer. A car, a carpool, or family in the area helps, but is not required.

5. How applying will work

  1. Fill out a short online application with a few short-answer questions.
  2. Attach your resume.
  3. The Steering Committee reads every application and may invite you to a short interview.

We are not accepting applications yet. Check back soon. We will post how to apply here when the program opens.

6. The research study

You apply only for the mentor role. As part of joining, you will be invited to also take part in a research study about the program. It is optional: you can be a mentor without joining the study. Saying no will not affect whether you are chosen, your stipend, grades, or course credit. Learn more about the study →

Contact Dr. Matthew Sparke, UC Santa Cruz · msparke@ucsc.edu
