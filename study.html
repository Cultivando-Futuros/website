The research study

An optional study about the program

Cultivando Futuros is a program first. The research study is an extra, optional part. There is no separate application for the study.

We are not accepting applications yet. We expect applications to open in February 2027. Notify me when the application opens →

1. What is the study?

We want to learn how Cultivando Futuros helps students grow. Our questions are:

  • Does the program help high school students feel more confident in science?
  • Does it help them see themselves as scientists?
  • Does it change their plans for college and careers?
  • How does being a mentor help college and graduate students grow?

What we learn can help other rural communities start programs like this one.

2. Is it required?

No. Taking part in the study is your choice. If you say no, you can still do everything in the program and still get your full stipend. Saying no never affects whether you are chosen. You can also change your mind and stop the study at any time.

3. Two ways to take part

1

Program only

all program activities and the full stipend.

2

Program and research study

the same activities and the same stipend. You also let us use your surveys, journal entries, and group conversations for the research.

4. How you join the study

You apply only for the program. As part of joining, a team member explains the study and invites you to take part.

5. What would I do?

  • Fill out a survey at the start and at the end of the program
  • Let us read the online journal (ePortfolio) that everyone in the program keeps
  • Maybe join a group conversation with other students about your experience

6. Who sees my answers?

  • Your mentors and supervisors will not see your survey or group conversation answers. Mentors help with ePortfolios as part of the program; confirm wording with the IRB.
  • In group conversations, the other students in the group will hear what you say.
  • When we share what we learned, we never use your name.
  • The main risk of the study is a loss of privacy. We take steps to keep your information private.

7. Who decides?

After people are chosen for the program, a team member explains the study. Students under 18 and their parents or guardians each decide. Students 18 and older, and college and graduate mentors, decide for themselves.

The study is reviewed by UC Berkeley's research ethics board (IRB).

Cultivando Futuros is a partnership of UC Berkeley, UC Santa Cruz, CSU Monterey Bay, Clínica de Salud del Valle de Salinas, and the Center for Community Advocacy.
